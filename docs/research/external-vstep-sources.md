# External VSTEP.3–5 practice sources: evaluation and licensing

Researched 2026-10-18 from primary sources (the publishers' own pages and terms of use). No test content (questions, passages, scripts, audio) is reproduced here or in the app. Only metadata, links and short quotes.

## Summary

| Category | Count | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Complete four-skill tests **in the app** (original, ours) | see `npm run content:validate` | Fully integrated, validated, with audio and explanations |
| External complete/near-complete four-skill sets | 6 | ZIM sample (1), Can Tho Univ. illustrative test (1), Anh Ngữ Vivian B1/B2/C1 sets (3) and online mock (1) |
| External single-skill collections | 4 | ACE of English: Reading (16 mocks), Listening (13 tests), Writing, Speaking libraries |
| Sources whose content **may be integrated** | **0** | Every source either prohibits copying or states no licence (copyright applies by default) |

**Conclusion:** All external material is **link-only**. The app's question bank stays original. External sets appear on the Resources page with their access conditions.

- Accessed 2026-10-18.

## Licensing conclusions
- None of the evaluated sources grants a licence or open licence (no Creative Commons, no government open-data terms) that would let us copy test content.
- Integrating any of it would need **written permission** from the rights holder (ZIM, Vivian, ACE) or from the publishing institution (CTU, ULIS). The app's content schema supports that case (`provenance.type = "licensed"`, `sourceId`, licence text) once permission exists.
- Until then, the app links to these resources (Resources page) and fills the gaps with **original** tests that follow the QĐ 729 format and are labelled as original practice material.
